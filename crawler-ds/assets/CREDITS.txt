Photographs used for the backgrounds (assets/bg) and the dungeon tiles
(assets/tiles). Each is a view framed, graded and colour-reduced out of a
360-degree panorama by tools/art/photo_bg.py; the panoramas themselves are
not in this repository, only where to fetch them.
